    the fact-check

    quick answer in seconds. a deeper model keeps digging behind it.

    press F on a flagged point and a small local model answers first. a slower, more careful model keeps working after that, with an eta, and it says so plainly if the two disagree.

    flagged claim: “the policy passed in 2020.”

    quick model · 1.4s

    partially true. the underlying bill passed in 2019. an amendment in 2020 changed its funding terms.

    ▲ contradiction · deep model · 4.1s

    disagrees with the quick answer's date. the deep model finds the 2020 figure only in the amended version, never in the original bill. worth raising before the point stands unchallenged.
